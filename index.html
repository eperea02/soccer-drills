    Drill Library

    Warm-up · ~10 min

    Box Dribbling

    Kids dribble freely in a box; on a whistle, everyone does a quick skill move before continuing.

    Game · ~10–15 min

    Zombie Coaches

    Kids dribble in a circle while "zombie" coaches — who can only walk — try to kick balls out.

    Game · ~10–15 min

    Buffalo Hunter

    Sharks-and-minnows style running game — coaches pass balls at runners crossing the field.

    Game · ~10 min

    Freeze Tag

    Kids dribble under pressure from taggers; get tagged and you freeze until a teammate frees you with a ball through the legs.

    Game · ~10–15 min

    Sharks & Minnows

    The classic dribbling game — minnows race a grid full of ball-hungry sharks, and every kid who loses their ball joins the hunt.

    Drill · ~10 min

    Red Light / Green Light

    The classic freeze game — kids dribble on "green light" and must stop the ball dead on "red light" or head back to the start.

    Game · ~10 min

    Clean Your Room

    Two teams, one big pile of balls — kick every ball off your side before the whistle.

    Drill · ~10 min

    Pull-Back Relay

    One move, repeated relentlessly — a pull-back at every cone until it's automatic.

    Game · ~10–15 min

    Numbers Game

    Two lines, matching numbers, one ball in the middle — call a number and watch two kids race to win it.

    Drill · ~10 min

    Relay Races

    Sprint to a cone, round it, and finish a rolled-in ball — fitness and finishing in one rep.

    Game · ~10 min

    Head or Catch

    A toss-and-react game around a circle — simple at first, then the coach flips head and catch to scramble everyone's instincts.

    Game · ~15–20 min

    World Cup

    Small teams pick a country, play short timed matches on a full-size goal, and finish practice with a real tournament final.

    Drill · ~10 min

    1v1's

    Two tiny goals, one rolled-in ball — first kid to score (or knock it out) wins the round.

    Game · ~10–15 min

    Handball

    No feet allowed — pass hand to hand and land a throw on a teammate standing in the endzone to score.

    Game · ~10 min

    Italian PK's

    Miss your PK and you're the new keeper — let in three straight and you're out of the game.

    Game · ~10 min

    King of the Ring

    Every kid dribbles their own ball inside a ring, shielding it while trying to knock everyone else's out — last ball standing wins.

    Game · ~10 min

    Ouch!

    Kids strike balls at a moving target — and that target is the coach. Every hit earns an "Ouch!" for the group.

    Drill · ~10–15 min

    Gate Passing

    Score points by threading passes through scattered cone gates — the more gates open, the more you have to look up.

    Drill · ~5–10 min

    1, 2, 3 Dribble

    Call a number, kids do that many touches, then straight back to dribbling — simple enough for the very first practice.
