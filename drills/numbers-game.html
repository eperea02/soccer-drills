Soccer game · reaction & 1v1 battles · ~10–15 min · works best on two smaller fields

Numbers Game

Two lines, matching numbers, one ball in the middle — react fast and win the race.

Split the group into two even teams facing each other in lines, and give every kid on each team a matching number. Drop a ball in the middle. Call out a number, and the two kids who share it sprint in to battle for the ball. Whoever gets there first controls it — everyone else is watching and waiting for their number to be called.

The setup

  • Kid waiting in line (with their number)
  • Kid sprinting to the ball
  • Loose ball in the middle
  • Direction of the sprint

How to play

  1. Coach calls out a number.
  2. The player with that number from each team sprints to the ball in the middle.
  3. The two battle 1v1 to win the ball and get it back to their own line, or score depending on the setup.
  4. Everyone resets, and the coach calls the next number.
  5. Rotate through numbers so every kid gets multiple reps.

Coaching tips

  • With a big group, run two smaller fields side by side instead of one long line — a big single line is tough to control and slows everyone's reps down.
  • Call numbers out of order so kids can't predict their turn.
  • Mix in calling two numbers at once for a quick 2v2 wrinkle once the basic version is easy.
Learned the hard way: two smaller fields beat one long line every time — split the group before the lines get unruly.
