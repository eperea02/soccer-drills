Clean Your Room

Two teams, one big pile of balls, and a simple job — get every ball off your side before time's up.

Split the field in half and scatter a big pile of balls evenly across both sides. Split the kids into two teams, one per half. On "go," everyone kicks balls from their side onto the opponent's side as fast as they can — no crossing the line to grab one. Whoever has fewer balls on their side when time's called wins the round.

The field

  • Kid kicking a ball across
  • Direction of the kick
  • Loose ball on the field
  • Cone marking the boundary

How to play

  1. Cone off a rectangular field and mark a center line, then scatter a big pile of balls evenly across both halves.
  2. Split the group into two teams, one per half.
  3. On "go," each team kicks balls from their own side over the center line onto the opponent's side.
  4. No one is allowed to step over the center line to grab a ball.
  5. Play for a set time, around 60–90 seconds.
  6. When time's called, everyone freezes immediately.
  7. Count the balls on each side — the team with fewer balls on their side wins that round.

Coaching tips

  • Run several short rounds back to back rather than one long one — the energy is highest right at the start.
  • Works with any group size; just add more balls for bigger groups.
  • Great as a warm-up or a cool-down finisher.
