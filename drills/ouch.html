Soccer game · striking accuracy · ~10 min · any group size

Ouch!

Kids strike balls at a moving target — and that target is the coach.

Cone off a grid. Every kid starts inside with a ball; one or two coaches jog around inside without one. Kids try to strike their ball to tag a coach below the knee — and every hit earns a yelled "Ouch!" and a point for the group. Simple, silly, and it sneaks in a ton of striking-accuracy reps without anyone noticing they're "practicing."

The grid

  • Kid striking toward the coach
  • Direction of the strike
  • Cone marking the grid
  • Coach (the moving target)

How to play

  1. Cone off a grid sized for your group. Every kid starts inside with a ball. One or two coaches (or older helpers) start inside without a ball.
  2. Coaches jog around inside the grid.
  3. Kids try to strike their ball to hit a coach, below the knee.
  4. Every time a coach gets hit, they yell "Ouch!" — that's a point for whoever struck it (or for the whole group, coach's choice).
  5. If kids are struggling to connect, the coach can slow to a walk, or stop in place for a few seconds to give everyone a chance.
  6. Play for a set time and see how many "Ouches" the group can rack up together.

Coaching tips

  • Track one team total instead of individual scores to keep it collaborative rather than competitive.
  • Rotate which coach is "it" every couple of minutes so it stays fresh.
  • Emphasize accuracy over power — a controlled pass that connects counts just as much as a hard strike.
