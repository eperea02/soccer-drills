Soccer game · coaches vs. kids · 10–15 min · any group size

Zombie Coaches

A dribbling-under-pressure game where the coaches are the "zombies" — slow, but relentless.

Every kid dribbles inside a circle or box while one or two "zombie" coaches try to kick balls out. The zombies can only walk, never run — so the whole game hinges on kids out-maneuvering a defender they should easily be able to escape.

The circle

  • Kid dribbling a ball
  • Direction they're moving
  • Cone marking the boundary
  • Zombie coach (walking only)

How to play

  1. Mark a circle or box — small enough to keep everyone close together.
  2. Every kid starts inside with a ball, dribbling around.
  3. One or more coaches stand inside as the "zombies."
  4. Kids dribble around inside the circle or box, keeping control of their ball.
  5. The zombie coaches walk around the area trying to kick balls out of it. Coaches can only walk — they can never run.
  6. If a kid's ball gets kicked out of the circle, they're out for that round.
  7. Play until one or two kids remain, then reset for another round.

Variations & coaching tips

  • When a kid's ball gets kicked out, you can either sit them out until the next round, or turn them into a zombie too (fun, but harder to enforce the walking-only rule with more zombies on the field).
  • If rounds are dragging on, shrink the box — it forces faster decisions and more contact.
  • Remind coaches to actually walk; the whole game hinges on kids being able to out-maneuver a "slow" defender.
Works the same in a square or a circle — use whichever is faster to mark out with the cones you've got.
