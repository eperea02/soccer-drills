Zombie Coaches

A dribbling-under-pressure game where the coaches are the "zombies" — slow, but relentless.

Setup

  • Mark a circle or box — small enough to keep everyone close together.
  • Every kid starts inside with a ball, dribbling around.
  • One or more coaches stand inside as the "zombies."

How to play

  1. Kids dribble around inside the circle or box, keeping control of their ball.
  2. The zombie coaches walk around the area trying to kick balls out of the circle. Coaches can only walk — they can never run.
  3. If a kid's ball gets kicked out of the circle, they're out for that round.
  4. Play until one or two kids remain, then reset for another round.

Variations & coaching tips

  • When a kid's ball gets kicked out, you can either sit them out until the next round, or turn them into a zombie too (fun, but harder to enforce the walking-only rule with more zombies on the field).
  • If rounds are dragging on, shrink the box — it forces faster decisions and more contact.
  • Remind coaches to actually walk; the whole game hinges on kids being able to out-maneuver a "slow" defender.
