Soccer game · finishing under pressure · ~10 min · big goal

Italian PK's

Miss, and you're in goal. Get scored on three straight times, and you're out.

One shooter, one keeper, one big goal. Every miss sends the shooter straight into goal to face the next kid in line — and every keeper who lets in three in a row is out of the game entirely. There's always something on the line, even in a simple penalty-kick line.

The setup

  • Shooter taking the kick
  • Ball in flight toward goal
  • Keeper in goal
  • Kids waiting in line

How to play

  1. The first shooter in line takes a penalty kick against the keeper.
  2. If the shooter scores, they go to the back of the shooting line and the keeper stays in goal.
  3. If the shooter misses (keeper saves it or it goes wide), the shooter becomes the new keeper and the old keeper joins the back of the shooting line.
  4. Keep a running count of saves for whoever's in goal.
  5. If a keeper gets scored on three times in a row, they're out of the game for that round; a fresh keeper steps in.

Coaching tips

  • Keep the shooting line moving briskly so no one waits too long between turns.
  • Great for finishing technique under a bit of real pressure and stakes.
  • Consider a small consequence (or just bragging rights) for whoever gets "out" to keep it fun rather than discouraging.
Simple enough to run with zero setup beyond a ball and a goal — and somehow nobody ever wants to leave the line.
