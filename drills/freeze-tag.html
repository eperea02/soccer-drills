Freeze Tag

A dribbling tag game where getting tagged doesn't mean sitting out — a teammate can free you.

Everyone dribbles inside a marked area while a couple of taggers hunt without a ball. Get tagged (or have your ball kicked away) and you freeze on the spot, legs apart, until a teammate dribbles their ball through the tunnel between your legs to set you free. No one sits out for long — it's constant movement for taggers and dribblers alike.

The area

  • Kid dribbling a ball
  • Direction they're moving
  • Frozen kid, legs apart
  • Cone marking the boundary
  • Tagger (no ball)

How to play

  1. Cone off an area sized for the group. Every kid starts inside with a ball.
  2. Pick 2 taggers (coaches or kids) who start without a ball.
  3. Everyone else dribbles freely inside the area, keeping the ball close.
  4. Taggers move through the area trying to tag a dribbler or kick their ball away.
  5. A tagged kid freezes immediately — stands still with legs apart, ball at their feet.
  6. A frozen kid is freed the moment another dribbler pushes their ball through the frozen kid's open legs (a "tunnel").
  7. Play continues on a timer; swap in new taggers every round or two.

Coaching tips

  • Rotate taggers often so no one feels stuck chasing the whole time.
  • Shrink the area to make tagging easier if dribblers are too spread out.
  • Encourage frozen kids to call out for a rescue — it keeps everyone engaged even while frozen.
Also doubles as a quick pre-practice warm-up — no setup beyond a few cones and a couple of volunteers to chase.
