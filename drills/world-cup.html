Soccer game · tournament finale · ~15–20 min · full-size goal

World Cup

Small teams, real countries, a full goal and keeper — practice ends with an actual tournament.

Split into teams of two (or three, depending on numbers), and let each team pick a country to play as. Teams play short, timed matches on a full-size goal with a keeper, working through the group before a final decides the winner. Every kid gets goal time, shot time, and bragging rights on the line.

The setup

  • Attacking player
  • Keeper in goal
  • Ball in play
  • Direction of a pass or shot

How to play

  1. Teams play short, timed matches — 3 to 5 minutes each — against a rotating set of opponents.
  2. Track wins informally as teams work through the group.
  3. Rotate keepers between matches so everyone gets goal time.
  4. Once every team has played, the top two records meet in a short final.
  5. Crown a champion and reset for another "tournament" another day.

Coaching tips

  • Keep matches short so everyone gets lots of touches and turns in goal rather than one long, tiring game.
  • Great as a finale — save it for the last chunk of practice.
  • Letting kids pick their own country adds a surprising amount of energy for very little setup.
No trophy required — bragging rights until next practice are apparently more than enough motivation.
