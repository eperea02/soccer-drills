Soccer game · dribbling under pressure · ~10–15 min · any group size

Sharks & Minnows

The classic — minnows dribble across open water while sharks in the middle try to knock their ball away.

Cone off a grid. All the "minnows" line up on one end, each with a ball. One or two "sharks" wait in the middle without a ball. On "go," minnows try to dribble to the far end without a shark kicking their ball out. Lose your ball, and you become a shark — so every round the water gets more dangerous.

The grid

  • Minnow dribbling a ball
  • Direction they're moving
  • Cone marking the grid
  • Shark hunting in the middle

How to play

  1. On "go," minnows dribble from their end toward the far end of the grid.
  2. Sharks move through the middle trying to poke or kick a minnow's ball out of the grid.
  3. Any minnow whose ball gets knocked out becomes a shark for the next round.
  4. Minnows who reach the far end safely wait there (or dribble back on the next round).
  5. Repeat rounds — more sharks each time — until one or two minnows remain; crown them winners and reset.

Coaching tips

  • Keep the grid width adjustable to balance difficulty as sharks multiply.
  • Let eliminated minnows back in after a round or two if the game is moving fast, so no one waits too long.
  • Remind sharks they can only play the ball, not the player.
