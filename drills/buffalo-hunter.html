Buffalo Hunter

A sharks-and-minnows style running game, built around passing accuracy for the coaches "hunting" on the sides.

Two lines of cones form a lane. Coaches line the outside with balls, and the kids sprint across, end to end, while the coaches try to touch runners with a passed ball. Get touched, and you join the hunters — so every round has fewer runners left dodging more balls.

The lane

  • Kid running (no ball)
  • Direction they're running
  • Cone marking the lane
  • Coach passing a ball

How to play

  1. Set up two lines of cones facing each other, forming the running lane.
  2. Coaches line up outside the cones on both sides, each with a ball to pass.
  3. Kids line up at one end of the lane, without a ball.
  4. On "go," the kids run from one end of the lane to the other.
  5. As they run through, the coaches on the sidelines pass balls at the runners, trying to touch them.
  6. Any kid touched by a ball is "out" — they join the coaches on the sideline for the next round.
  7. Repeat each round: more and more kids move from running to passing on the sidelines, while fewer remain running through the middle.

Coaching tips

  • Start with only coaches passing so the first round or two isn't too hard, then let the game snowball as more kids join the sidelines.
  • Great for passing accuracy on the sideline and change-of-direction / acceleration for the runners.
  • Keep the lane width adjustable — narrow it if runners are getting through too easily.
Works well as a standalone conditioning game too — even without the "out" rule, sprinting the lane under incoming passes is a workout on its own.
