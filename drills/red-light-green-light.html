Soccer drill · ball control under pressure · ~10 min · any group size

Red Light / Green Light

The playground classic, but every kid's racing the finish line with a ball at their feet.

Kids line up with a ball at one end; the coach stands at the far end calling the shots. "Green light" and everyone dribbles toward the coach. "Red light" and everyone must stop the ball dead, instantly. Still moving when the light turns red, and it's back to the start — first kid to reach the coach in full control wins.

The lane

  • Kid dribbling toward the coach
  • Direction of travel
  • Cone marking the start line
  • Coach calling the lights

How to play

  1. Coach calls "Green light!" and everyone dribbles toward the coach.
  2. Coach calls "Red light!" (or turns around) at random moments.
  3. On red light, everyone must instantly stop the ball completely still under their foot.
  4. Anyone still moving the ball, or who loses control on the stop, goes back to the start line.
  5. First kid to reach the coach without getting sent back wins — and can be the next caller.

Coaching tips

  • Mix in quick fake-outs between calls to build real reaction speed.
  • Emphasize a clean, controlled stop over just freezing in place — that's the actual skill being trained.
  • Let the winner call the next round to keep engagement high.
No prop budget needed — just cones, balls, and a coach willing to yell "red light" with conviction.
