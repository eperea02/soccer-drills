Soccer drill · fitness & finishing · ~10 min · two even lines

Relay Races

A straight sprint, a turn around a cone, and a rolled-in ball to finish it off with a shot.

Two lines face off on either side of the goal. Each runner sprints out to a cone, rounds it, and races back toward goal — and just as they arrive, the coach rolls a ball out in front of them to finish. It's a fitness drill and a finishing drill in one rep.

The setup

  • Kid sprinting the loop
  • Direction of the run
  • Turn-around cone
  • Ball rolled in to finish

How to run it

  1. First kid in each line sprints out to the far cone.
  2. They round the cone and sprint back toward the goal.
  3. As they get close, the coach rolls a ball out in front of them.
  4. The runner attacks the rolling ball and finishes on goal.
  5. They join the back of the line (or step out), and the next kid goes — first line through everyone wins.

Coaching tips

  • Adjust the cone distance to match the group's fitness level — the point is a hard sprint, not a jog.
  • Time the rolled ball so it arrives right as they're closing in, not too early or too late.
  • Alternate which line shoots first so it stays fair and competitive.
Simple enough to run with one ball and two cones — the finishing twist is what keeps it from feeling like just another sprint set.
