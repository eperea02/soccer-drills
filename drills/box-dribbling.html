Box Dribbling

A simple warm-up that keeps kids moving with a ball while sneaking in skill reps.

Setup

  • Mark out a box (cones work fine) sized for your group — big enough that kids aren't bumping into each other, small enough that they stay close together.
  • Every kid starts inside the box with a ball.

How to play

  1. Kids dribble freely around the box, keeping the ball close.
  2. Every time the coach blows the whistle, everyone stops dribbling and performs a quick skill move — for example 5 toe touches, a pull-back, a juggle, or a trap.
  3. After finishing the move, kids pick the ball back up and continue dribbling around the box.
  4. Repeat, calling out a different move (or letting the coach demonstrate) each time the whistle blows.

Coaching tips

  • Cycle through several different moves so it stays fresh — toe touches, pull-backs, juggles, traps, sole rolls.
  • Great opener: low pressure, gets everyone touching the ball right away, and doubles as a warm-up.
  • Shrink the box if kids need to slow down and control the ball more; widen it if they need room to work on speed.
