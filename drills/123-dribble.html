Soccer drill · ball mastery filler · ~5–10 min · any group size

1, 2, 3 Dribble

Call a number, kids do that many touches, then straight back to dribbling — simple enough for the very first practice.

Cone off a box and let everyone loose with a ball, dribbling wherever there's space — just like Box Dribbling. The twist here is even simpler: call out a number and a move, like "3 toe touches!", and every kid stops to do exactly that many reps before going right back to dribbling. No whistle, no elimination, no waiting — just counting and touches, which makes it perfect for the very youngest players still learning to follow along.

The box

  • Kid dribbling a ball
  • Direction they're moving
  • Cone marking the box
  • Kid mid-count on their called move

How to run it

  1. Everyone dribbles freely inside the area.
  2. Coach calls out a number and a move together, like "2 pull-backs!" or "4 toe touches!"
  3. Each kid stops and performs that many reps of the called move.
  4. After finishing their count, everyone goes back to free dribbling.
  5. Repeat with new numbers and moves, mixing it up so kids stay listening.

Coaching tips

  • Start with small numbers (1–3) so the youngest players succeed right away, then build up as the group gets comfortable.
  • Great low-key filler between bigger games, or as a settling-down activity at the very start of practice.
  • Pairs naturally with Box Dribbling's whistle format — you can combine the two once a group knows both.
Works fine even for kids who can't count very high yet — just keep the numbers low and let them grow into it.
