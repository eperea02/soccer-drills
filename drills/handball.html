Handball

No feet allowed — just hands, quick passes, and constant movement to get open in the endzone.

Mark a square field with an endzone on each end. Split into two teams. Kids move the ball with their hands only, passing it teammate to teammate — but the second the ball's in your hands, your feet have to stop. Score by completing a pass to a teammate standing in the opponent's endzone.

The field

  • Player passing
  • Cone marking the field/endzone
  • Teammate waiting in the endzone
  • The ball in flight

How to play

  1. Kids pass the ball to teammates using only their hands.
  2. The moment a kid catches the ball, they must stop moving — no walking or running while holding it, though pivoting in place is fine.
  3. Defenders can intercept passes or block throwing lanes, but can't grab the ball out of a teammate's hands.
  4. To score, a team completes a pass to a teammate standing inside the opponent's endzone.
  5. Reset after a score (or a turnover past midfield) and keep playing.

Coaching tips

  • Add a "no passing straight back to whoever passed it to you" rule once the basics click — it forces more movement off the ball.
  • Great low-impact game for practicing spacing, give-and-go timing, and communication without any dribbling involved.
  • Works with any field size — just shrink it for a smaller or younger group.
Feet-free, but not brain-free — the second everyone's frozen mid-catch, spacing is the only thing that still moves the ball.
