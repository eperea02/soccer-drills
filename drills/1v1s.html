Soccer drill · competitive 1v1 · ~10 min · best with half the group

1v1's

Two tiny goals, one rolled-in ball, and a straight-up battle to see who scores first.

Set up a small field with two pug goals close together, facing opposite ways. Two lines face off across the field. The coach rolls a ball into the middle, and the first kid from each line battles 1v1 — attacking one goal while defending the other. First to score, or first to knock it out of play, and the next pair steps up.

The setup

  • Kid battling 1v1
  • The ball in play
  • Direction of an attack
  • The two goals

How to play

  1. The first kid from each line steps up to the middle.
  2. The coach rolls a ball into the space between them.
  3. Both kids battle 1v1 — each attacking one goal while defending the other.
  4. If the ball goes out of play past either side, the round ends and both kids go to the back of their lines.
  5. The next pair steps up immediately to keep the line moving.

Coaching tips

  • Run this with roughly half the group at a time so the lines don't get long and stale.
  • Great drill for shielding, quick take-ons, and competitive touches under pressure.
  • Keep the field small — the closer the goals, the more decisive the battles.
No whistle needed — the next pair is already walking up before you can even call the score.
