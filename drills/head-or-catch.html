Head or Catch

A simple toss game that turns into a real reaction test the moment you flip the rule.

Everyone forms a circle with the coach in the middle. One by one, the coach tosses the ball to a kid, calling "Head!" or "Catch!" while it's in the air. At first, kids do exactly what's called. Once that's too easy, flip it — say "Head" and they have to catch, say "Catch" and they have to head it.

The circle

  • Kid waiting in the circle
  • Coach in the center
  • Ball in the air mid-toss

How to play

  1. Coach tosses the ball to one kid in the circle.
  2. While the ball is in the air, the coach calls out either "Head!" or "Catch!"
  3. The kid does exactly what's called — heads the ball back or catches it — then it goes back to the coach.
  4. Rotate around the circle so every kid gets a turn.
  5. Once the group has it down, flip the rule: whatever the coach calls, kids must do the opposite.

Coaching tips

  • Keep the tosses soft and accurate — the challenge should be the reaction, not the catch itself.
  • Great as a wind-down at the end of practice; low intensity but keeps focus sharp.
  • Bring back the "opposite" version later in the season once the basic version gets easy again.
Simple enough to run with a ball and a circle of kids — the whole game is really just you deciding when to lie.
