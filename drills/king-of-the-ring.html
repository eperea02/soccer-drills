Soccer game · ball shielding & control · ~10 min · no teams needed

King of the Ring

Protect your own ball, knock out everyone else's — last ball standing wins.

Cone off a ring and let every kid loose with a ball. There are no teams and no separate defenders — every kid is both attacker and defender at once, dribbling to protect their own ball while trying to poke another kid's ball out of the ring. Lose your ball, and you're out for that round; keep it, and the ring just keeps shrinking with fewer balls left to dodge.

The ring

  • Kid dribbling and shielding their ball
  • Direction they're moving
  • Cone marking the ring
  • Ball knocked out of the ring

How to play

  1. Cone off a square ring sized for the group — smaller for younger or fewer kids. Every kid starts inside with a ball.
  2. On "go," everyone dribbles freely inside the ring, keeping their own ball under close control.
  3. While protecting your own ball, try to poke or kick another kid's ball out of the ring.
  4. A ball that gets kicked out — or that rolls out because a kid lost control of it — is out for that round; that kid steps to the sideline.
  5. Keep playing until just one ball (and kid) remains inside the ring.
  6. Crown them "King of the Ring" and reset for another round.

Coaching tips

  • Shrink the ring as fewer kids remain — it keeps the ending exciting instead of two kids wandering a huge empty space.
  • Remind everyone they can only play the ball, never push or grab another kid.
  • It's a great low-equipment game — just cones and one ball per kid, no teams to organize.
Works with almost any group size since there's no team-splitting to organize — just cones, balls, and everyone for themselves.
